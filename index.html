Full syllabus

Seventeen weeks, zero to advanced

Week 00 Foundations

Lab Readiness & Embedded Foundations

Set up a safe, legal home lab and build the electronics, embedded-OS, networking, and scripting baseline the rest of the course assumes.

 Week 01 Core

What IoT/CPS Security Protects

Reframe the CIA triad around safety and availability, and learn to threat-model systems that touch the physical world.

 Week 02 Core

Device Identity, Access & Embedded OS Hardening

Harden the embedded OS layer and give every device a verifiable identity before it ever joins a network.

 Week 03 Core

Applied Cryptography for Constrained Devices

Do cryptography correctly on hardware with tiny CPUs, weak entropy sources, and thin power budgets.

 Week 04 Core

Secure Firmware & Embedded Software Engineering

Build the secure development lifecycle for firmware: from requirements through dependencies to a safe OTA pipeline.

 Week 05 Advanced

Memory Safety & Firmware Exploitation Foundations

Go under the hood of memory corruption, mitigations, and fuzzing as they apply to bare-metal and RTOS firmware.

 Week 06 Advanced

Hardware Security & Side-Channel Foundations

Study the silicon and board level: secure boot chains, debug interfaces, side channels, and tamper resistance.

 Week 07 Core

IoT & Wireless Network Security

Cover the wireless and application-layer protocols that make IoT different from ordinary IP networking.

 Week 08 Practical

Midterm Practical: IoT Attack Surface & Defense Review

An integrated practical assessment across a fictional smart-building deployment: identify attack surface, prioritize risk, and produce a defense plan.

 Week 09 Core

Endpoint & Fleet Security for IoT Devices

Manage security at fleet scale: hardening, patching, malware concepts, telemetry, and ransomware-class availability attacks.

 Week 10 Core

Cloud, Platforms & DevSecOps for IoT

Secure the cloud side of IoT: provisioning, identity governance, edge compute, pipelines, and telemetry-driven monitoring.

 Week 11 Core

Security Operations & Detection for IoT/OT

Build detection and SOC workflows tuned to IoT/OT telemetry, alerting, and investigation patterns.

 Week 12 Core

Incident Response & Digital Forensics for IoT/OT

Respond to and investigate incidents that involve firmware, flash memory, and physical-world evidence.

 Week 13 Advanced

Offensive Security & Hardware Pentesting

Practice authorized offensive testing against devices, firmware, and RF — scoped, safe, and well documented.

 Week 14 Core

Governance, Compliance, Privacy & Risk for IoT

Navigate the regulatory, privacy, and third-party risk landscape that now governs connected products.

 Week 15 Advanced

Industrial, Critical Infrastructure & Emerging Topics

Go deep on OT/ICS/SCADA, zero trust at scale, post-quantum readiness, edge AI, and secure-by-design futures.

 Week 16 Capstone

Capstone

Bring every layer together: assess, defend, investigate, and present a full IoT security program for a fictional fleet.
